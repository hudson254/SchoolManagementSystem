## 1. Introduction

Welcome to the School Management System. This guide will help you navigate and use all the features available to students in the system.

### 1.1 Purpose

The School Management System is designed to provide students with a central platform for managing their academic journey, including course enrollment, accessing learning materials, submitting assignments, and tracking academic progress.

### 1.2 System Requirements

- Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari)
- Internet connection (or LAN access)
- PDF reader for viewing lecture notes
- Word processor for viewing assignments

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 Creating an Account

> **Note:** If you already have an account, proceed to Section 2.2.

**Step 1:** Navigate to the School Management System login page.  
**Step 2:** Click on the **"Create Account"** button.  
**Step 3:** Fill in the registration form:

| Field | Description | Required |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| First Name | Your first name | Yes |
| Last Name | Your last name | Yes |
| Email | Your email address | Yes |
| Password | Minimum 8 characters with uppercase, lowercase, number, and special character | Yes |
| Confirm Password | Re-enter your password | Yes |
| Phone Number | Your contact number | Yes |
| Organization | Your school/organization name | No |

**Step 4:** Click **"Register"**.  
**Step 5:** You will receive a verification email. Click the link in the email to verify your account.  
**Step 6:** Wait for account approval (if required by your institution).

### 2.2 Logging In

**Step 1:** Navigate to the login page.  
**Step 2:** Enter your email and password.  
**Step 3:** Optionally check **"Remember Me"** to stay logged in.  
**Step 4:** Click **"Sign In"**.

![Login Page Placeholder]

### 2.3 Password Reset

If you forget your password:

**Step 1:** Click **"Forgot Password?"** on the login page.  
**Step 2:** Enter your registered email address.  
**Step 3:** Click **"Send Reset Link"**.  
**Step 4:** Check your email for a password reset link.  
**Step 5:** Click the link and enter your new password.  
**Step 6:** Confirm your new password and click **"Reset Password"**.

### 2.4 Dashboard Overview

After logging in, you will see the dashboard:

| Section | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Statistics | Quick view of enrolled units, assignments, grades |
| Upcoming Deadlines | Assignments due soon |
| Recent Activity | Latest system notifications |
| Quick Actions | Access to common tasks |

## 3. Profile Management

**Step 1:** Navigate to Profile.  
**Step 2:** Click **"Change Password"**.  
**Step 3:** Enter your current password.

**Step 6:** Click **"Change Password"**.

## 4. Academic Management

### 4.1 Browsing Courses

**Step 1:** Navigate to **"Courses"** in the sidebar.  
**Step 2:** View the list of available courses.  
**Step 3:** Use the search bar to find specific courses.  
**Step 4:** Click on a course to view details:

- Course description
- Duration
- Total credits
- Programmes offered

### 4.2 Selecting Courses

**Step 1:** Navigate to **"Courses"**.  
**Step 2:** Click on a course of interest.  
**Step 3:** Review the course details.  
**Step 4:** Click **"Select Course"** to enroll.

### 4.3 Enrolling in Units

**Step 1:** Navigate to **"Units"**.  
**Step 2:** Browse available units.  
**Step 3:** Click on a unit to view details:

- Description
- Credits
- Contact hours
- Prerequisites

**Step 4:** Click **"Enroll"** to register for the unit.

> **Warning:** Ensure you meet all prerequisites before enrolling in a unit.

### 4.4 Viewing Approved Enrollment

**Step 1:** Navigate to **"Enrollments"**.  
**Step 2:** View your approved enrollments.  
**Step 3:** Check the status of each enrollment:

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Enrolled | Successfully enrolled |
| In Progress | Unit is ongoing |
| Completed | Unit completed |
| Dropped | Enrollment dropped |

## 5. Learning Materials

### 5.1 Accessing Lecture Notes

**Step 1:** Navigate to **"Units"**.  
**Step 2:** Select a unit you are enrolled in.  
**Step 3:** Click on **"Lecture Notes"**.  
**Step 4:** View the list of available notes:

- Title
- Upload date
- Lecturer name

**Step 5:** Click on a note to view it.

### 5.2 Downloading Learning Materials

**Step 1:** Navigate to the lecture notes section.  
**Step 2:** Locate the material you want to download.  
**Step 3:** Click the **"Download"** icon.

Supported file formats:
- PDF documents
- Word documents (.doc, .docx)
- PowerPoint presentations (.ppt, .pptx)
- Excel files (.xls, .xlsx)
- Images (.jpg, .png)

## 6. Assignments

### 6.1 Viewing Assignments

**Step 1:** Navigate to **"Assignments"**.  
**Step 2:** View the list of assignments:

- Title
- Unit
- Due Date
- Status
- Score (if graded)

**Step 3:** Click on an assignment for details:

- Description
- Instructions
- Attachments
- Max Score
- Weight

**Step 1:** Navigate to the assignment details.  
**Step 2:** Click **"Submit Assignment"**.  
**Step 3:** Upload your file:

| Requirement | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| File Format | PDF, DOC, DOCX, PPT, PPTX, ZIP |

> **Warning:** Late submissions may incur penalties. Check the assignment deadline and late submission policy.

### 6.3 Tracking Submission Status

**Step 1:** Navigate to **"Assignments"**.  
**Step 2:** Check the status column:

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Submitted | Assignment submitted |
| Pending | Awaiting grading |
| Graded | Assignment graded |

### 6.4 Viewing Feedback

**Step 1:** Navigate to **"Assignments"**.  
**Step 2:** Click on a graded assignment.  
**Step 3:** View feedback:

- Score
- Comments
- Graded Date

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions

### 11.1 Login Issues

**Q:** I can't log in. What should I do?  
**A:** Check your email and password. If you forgot your password, use the "Forgot Password" feature. If you're still having issues, contact IT support.

### 11.2 Enrollment Issues

**Q:** I can't enroll in a unit. Why?  
**A:** Possible reasons:
- You haven't met the prerequisites
- The unit is full
- Registration is closed

### 11.3 Assignment Submission

**Q:** I submitted my assignment, but it's not showing as submitted. What should I do?  
**A:** Wait a few minutes and refresh the page. If it still doesn't show, resubmit. If the problem persists, contact your lecturer.

### 11.4 Grades

**Q:** When will my grades be available?  
**A:** Grades are typically posted within 2 weeks of the assignment deadline or exam. Check with your lecturer for specific timelines.

## 12. Troubleshooting

### 12.1 Browser Issues

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Page not loading | Clear cache, try a different browser |
| Buttons not working | Refresh the page, ensure JavaScript is enabled |
| Login redirecting | Check your credentials, ensure cookies are enabled |

### 12.2 File Upload Issues

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| File too large | Compress the file, or contact support |
| Unsupported format | Convert to a supported format (PDF recommended) |
| Upload failed | Try again, check your internet connection |
