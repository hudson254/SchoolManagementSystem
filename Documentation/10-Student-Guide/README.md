## Getting Started

The Student Portal allows you to manage your academic life including course enrollment, accessing learning materials, submitting assignments, tracking grades, and viewing your academic progress.

---

## Registration

### Creating a Student Account
1. Navigate to the system login page
2. Click **Register** or **Create Account**
3. Fill in your details:
   - Full name
   - Email address
   - Phone number
   - ID/Passport number
4. Create a username (or use the auto-generated one)
5. Create a password (must meet requirements)
6. Select the course you wish to enroll in
7. Click **Submit**

### After Registration
- Your account status will be **Pending**
- A coordinator will review and approve your registration
- You will receive a notification when your account is approved
- If rejected, you will receive a reason and can re-register

---

## Login

### Logging In
1. Navigate to the login page
2. Enter your username and password
3. Click **Login**

### First-Time Login
1. If you received a temporary password, you will be prompted to change it
2. Create a new password (minimum 12 characters with uppercase, lowercase, digit, and special character)
3. Update your profile information

### Forgot Password
If you forget your password:
1. Click **Forgot Password** on the login page
2. Contact your coordinator or administrator to request a password reset
3. You will receive a temporary password
4. Log in and change your password immediately

## Profile Management

### Viewing Your Profile
1. Click on your name at the top right
2. Select **Profile**
3. View your:
   - Personal details
   - Contact information
   - Enrollment status
   - Course information

### Updating Your Profile
1. Navigate to **Profile** > **Edit**
2. Update your:
   - Phone number
   - Address
   - Profile picture
3. Click **Save**

> **⚠️ NOTE**: You cannot change your name, email, or username. Contact your coordinator for these changes.

## Course Enrollment

### Enrolling in a Course
1. Navigate to **Enrollment** > **Course Selection**
2. View available courses for the current academic period
3. Select the course you want to enroll in
4. Review the course details and units
5. Click **Enroll**
6. Your enrollment will be submitted for processing

### Enrollment Status
- **Pending**: Your enrollment is being processed
- **Active**: You are enrolled and can access course materials
- **Confirmed**: You have confirmed your enrollment
- **Dropped**: You are no longer enrolled
- **Completed**: You have completed the course

### Confirming Enrollment
1. Navigate to **Enrollment** > **Pending Confirmations**
2. Review your enrollment details
3. Click **Confirm** to accept the enrollment

### Dropping a Course
1. Navigate to **Enrollment** > **My Enrollments**
2. Find the course and click **Drop**
3. Confirm the drop request
4. A coordinator may need to approve the drop

### Returning Students
If you are a returning student:
1. Navigate to **Enrollment** > **Returning Student**
2. View your course history
3. Select units to re-enroll in
4. Submit your enrollment request

## Assignments

### Viewing Assignments
1. Navigate to **Assignments**
2. View all assignments for your enrolled units
3. Each assignment shows:
   - Title
   - Description
   - Due date and time
   - Maximum score
   - Weight
   - Status (Not Submitted, Submitted, Late, Graded)

### Submitting an Assignment
1. Navigate to **Assignments** > **Select Assignment**
2. Click **Submit**
3. Upload your file(s)
4. Add any comments (optional)
5. Click **Submit Assignment**

> **⚠️ WARNING**: Ensure you submit before the deadline. Late submissions may be marked as "Late".

### Viewing Submission Status
1. Navigate to **Assignments** > **My Submissions**
2. View the status of each submission:
   - **Submitted**: Awaiting grading
   - **Late**: Submitted after deadline
   - **Graded**: Score and feedback available

---

## Downloads

### Accessing Course Materials
1. Navigate to **My Units** > **Select Unit**
2. Click **Materials**
3. Browse available materials:
   - Lecture notes
   - Slides
   - Reading materials
   - Past papers
4. Click the file name to download

### Downloading Grades
1. Navigate to **Grades**
2. Click **Download Report**
3. Select format (PDF or Excel)
4. The report will be downloaded to your device

---

## Submissions

### Viewing Submission History
1. Navigate to **Assignments** > **Submission History**
2. View all your past submissions
3. Each entry shows:
   - Assignment name
   - Submission date
   - File(s) submitted
   - Grade (if graded)
   - Feedback (if provided)

### Re-submitting an Assignment
If the lecturer allows re-submissions:
1. Navigate to **Assignments** > **My Submissions**
2. Find the assignment and click **Re-submit**
3. Upload the new file(s)
4. Click **Submit**

---

## Grades

### Viewing Your Grades
1. Navigate to **Grades**
2. View your grades by:
   - Unit
   - Assessment type
   - Academic period

### Understanding Your Grades
The grade view shows:
- Unit name and code
- Assessment scores
- Weight for each assessment
- Weighted total
- Final grade
- Grade band (A, B, C, D, F)

### Grade Scale
| Grade | Range | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| A | 70-100% | Excellent |
| B | 60-69% | Good |
| C | 50-59% | Satisfactory |
| D | 40-49% | Pass |
| F | Below 40% | Fail |

### Downloading Transcript
1. Navigate to **Grades** > **Transcript**
2. Click **Download**
3. Select PDF format
4. The transcript includes all completed units and grades

## Password Changes

### Changing Your Password
1. Click on your name at the top right
2. Select **Change Password**
3. Enter:
   - Current password
   - New password (12+ characters)
   - Confirm new password
4. Click **Save**

### Password Requirements
- Minimum 12 characters
- At least one uppercase letter
- At least one lowercase letter
- At least one digit
- At least one special character
- At least 4 unique characters

## Certificates

### Viewing Your Certificates

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I know if my registration was approved?**
A: You will receive a notification. You can also check your account status on your profile page.

**Q: Can I change my course after enrollment?**
A: Contact your coordinator to discuss course changes.

**Q: What if I miss an assignment deadline?**
A: Submit as soon as possible. The system will mark it as "Late". Contact your lecturer if you have a valid reason.

**Q: How do I view my academic progress?**
A: Navigate to Grades to view your current scores and predicted grades.

**Q: What if I forget my password?**
A: Contact your coordinator or administrator to request a password reset.
